# UX and Interactions

## Timeline / Album Grid
- Infinite scroll timeline (newest -> oldest).
- Album grid shows all albums to owner.
- Public album view shows only the linked album.

## Selection
- Multi-select in timeline or album grid.
- On selection, show actions menu:
  - Add to album
  - Remove from album (when in album view)

## Viewer
- Left/right arrows for prev/next.
- Arrows appear on hover near edges and are clickable.

## Live Photos
- Hover plays the paired video inline with no sound.
- When opened in viewer, do not auto-play on hover.

## Video Viewer
- Video player lives in the viewer.
- Still image for the video can be viewed full-size with zoom.
